# Steady-State Behavior of Constant-Stepsize Stochastic Approximation

OpenReview: https://openreview.net/forum?id=m4TAzup6Yc
arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.13960

Clean-room CPU reproduction. Six anchored claims (12 possible points). Claims
1–5 are verified within finite d=8 contracts; Claim 6 is blocked by the
source-consistency and conjecture audit. The current evidence is stronger
than the historical 1D toy surface but does not prove universal theorems.
