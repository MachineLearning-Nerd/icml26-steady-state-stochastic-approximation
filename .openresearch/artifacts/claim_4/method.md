# Method — Claim 4

Evaluate two coordinate directions, the normalized all-ones direction, and an
alternating direction. For each, compare empirical and exact Gaussian tails at
four raw thresholds. Construct an exact two-sided 95% Clopper-Pearson interval
for each Bernoulli exceedance probability, then use the larger endpoint distance
from the fixed Gaussian target as the tail-gap upper confidence bound. Calibrate
the unknown multiplicative envelope only at alpha at least 0.02 and test it at
the two smaller held-out stepsizes.
