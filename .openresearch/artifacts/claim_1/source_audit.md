# Source audit — Claim 1

Source: Theorem 3.1 at HTML anchor `S3.Thmtheorem1` and Theorem 4.1 at
`S4.Thmtheorem1`. Theorem 3.1 depends on Assumptions 3.1–3.2 and a uniform
third moment of the centered stationary law. Theorem 4.1 replaces iid noise by
Assumption 4.1, the long-run covariance, bounded Poisson solutions, joint
stationarity, and a uniform fourth moment.

Both results are upper bounds for all sufficiently small alpha. They do not
assert monotonic finite-sample estimates, equality with the displayed rate, or
a fitted exponent. The target covariance solves the corresponding continuous
Lyapunov equation.
