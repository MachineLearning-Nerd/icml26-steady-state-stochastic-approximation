# Limitations — Claim 1

This is a rigorous multidimensional model instantiation, not a computer proof
of the theorem's universal quantifier. The W1 bracket can be wider by a factor
up to eight. Confidence intervals use four independent seed groups; within a
group, thinned samples come from independent parallel chains. The unknown paper
constant is tested through out-of-sample envelope stability rather than a full
reimplementation of every Stein constant.
