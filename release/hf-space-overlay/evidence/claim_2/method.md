# Method — Claim 2

The objective is globally smooth and strongly convex because each Hessian is
`q_i+s_i cos(x)` with `q_i>s_i`; its third derivative is bounded by `s_i`.
The model is nonlinear but separable, so it is not the vacuous exactly
Gaussian quadratic case. Stationary samples, W1 brackets, held-out envelopes,
uncertainty, and the negative control are generated by the fixed command.
